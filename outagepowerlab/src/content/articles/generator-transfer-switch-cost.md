This guide is based on two published cost guides, HomeGuide and Angi, read on November 27, 2026. Angi labels its figures as 2024 data. We have not installed a transfer switch ourselves, and this is not a wiring guide. Anything that connects to your panel is a job for a licensed electrician.

## Why you need one at all

A transfer switch (or an interlock kit) is what makes it safe to power your house from a generator. It guarantees that your home is connected to either the utility or the generator, never both. Without it, generator power can flow backward onto the utility lines, which is called backfeeding.

HomeGuide puts it plainly: connecting a portable generator to a house without a transfer switch or interlocking device "is illegal in many places due to fire and electrocution risk from backfeeding into the main power supply." Backfeeding also endangers the line workers trying to restore your power.

## Cost by type

| Type | Unit only | Installed | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interlock kit | $50 to $150 | $400 to $850 | Portable generator, lowest cost, panel must support it |
| Manual transfer switch | $200 to $800 | $400 to $1,300 | Portable generator, select circuits |
| Automatic transfer switch | $400 to $2,000 | $600 to $2,500 | Standby generator, switches without you |

Unit and installed figures are HomeGuide's. Angi gives the same installed ranges for manual ($400 to $1,300) and automatic ($600 to $2,500) switches, and a normal overall range of **$1,200 to $1,600**, averaging **$1,450**.

### Interlock kit

An interlock is a sliding metal plate on your main panel that lets only the main breaker or the generator breaker be on at one time. HomeGuide's breakdown:

- Interlock kit: $50 to $150
- Breaker, power inlet box, cord and supplies: $200 to $400
- Labor: $150 to $300
- **Total: $400 to $850**

It is usually the cheapest legal option, but it only works if a listed interlock exists for your panel model, and you have to switch it by hand.

### Manual transfer switch

A separate small panel, wired to the circuits you choose, such as the fridge, furnace and sump pump. You plug in the generator and flip the switches. Installed: **$400 to $1,300**.

### Automatic transfer switch

Standard with standby generators. It senses the outage, starts the generator and switches over in seconds, then switches back when the grid returns. Installed: **$600 to $2,500**. Many standby generators include one in the price.

## Cost by amperage

HomeGuide lists unit prices by switch size, before installation (add about **$200 to $500** for labor on a straightforward job):

| Switch size | Generator size it handles | Unit price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 30 amp | 7,500 to 8,000 W | $200 to $500 |
| 50 amp | 8,500 to 12,500 W | $260 to $900 |
| 100 amp | 10,000 to 24,000 W | $300 to $950 |
| 200 amp | 45,000 to 48,000 W | $680 to $1,500 |

Match the switch to your generator, not to your house. A 7,500 W portable generator needs a 30 amp setup, not a 200 amp switch. Our [generator size calculator](/generator-size-calculator/) helps you pick the generator first.

## Labor and permits

- **Electricians:** $50 to $130 an hour plus a call-out fee around $75 (HomeGuide), or $50 to $100 an hour (Angi)
- **Time:** two to four hours or more for most installs
- **Permit:** $50 to $350, according to both sources. HomeGuide says most areas require a permit and inspection for any electrical change.

## Which one should you get?

- **Standby generator:** an automatic transfer switch. It is part of the system, and often part of the price. See [what a whole-house generator costs](/how-much-does-a-whole-house-generator-cost/).
- **Portable generator, panel supports an interlock:** an interlock kit plus an inlet box is usually the cheapest code-compliant setup.
- **Portable generator, no interlock available or you want only a few circuits:** a manual transfer switch.

Whichever you choose, the generator itself still has to run outside, well away from the house. Read [why a generator is never safe in a garage](/can-you-run-a-generator-in-a-garage/).
