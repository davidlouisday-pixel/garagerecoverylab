This guide is based on Finnish sauna and tourism guidance, listed at the end. Cole uses a shared sauna at his gym four or more times a week, which is where these rules come up most.

## The short version

The Finnish Sauna Society says traditional sauna customs are built on respecting other people's peace in the sauna. Almost every rule below comes back to that.

## Before you go in

1. **Shower first.** Visit Jyväskylä, Visit Tampere and finland.fi all list this first. Nobody wants to sit next to your workout.
2. **Bring a towel to sit on.** Visit Jyväskylä says a seat towel is always needed, whatever else you wear. Sit so your sweat lands on your towel, not the bench.
3. **Check the dress code.** In Finland, single-sex and private saunas are often nude. Mixed public saunas usually expect swimwear, according to Visit Jyväskylä and Visit Tampere. US gyms almost always expect a swimsuit or a towel.
4. **Wear flip-flops** to and from a public sauna, as Visit Tampere suggests, but leave them outside the hot room if that is the custom.

## Inside the sauna

5. **Close the door quickly.** Every second it is open, heat escapes for everyone.
6. **Ask before adding steam.** Visit Jyväskylä says to check with the others before throwing water on the stones, and that the person nearest the bucket usually does it. Steam makes the room much hotter for everyone. Use a small amount and follow any posted limit.
7. **Let others have the top bench.** Visit Tampere says the hottest upper spots go to the people who were waiting first.
8. **Keep your voice down.** Quiet conversation is fine. Shouting is not, and some saunas have quiet hours.
9. **No phones.** Visit Tampere lists phones as something to leave outside. It also protects everyone's privacy.
10. **No food,** and don't bring in anything that smells strong.

## Leaving and after

11. **Shower after,** before using a pool or cold plunge. Visit Tampere says to shower before and after.
12. **Leave the space ready** for the next person: towel out, door closed, nothing left behind.

## Gym sauna rules worth adding

These are not Finnish rules, but they matter in a gym sauna. They are our suggestions:

- **No stretching across the benches** when it is busy.
- **Don't shave, scrub or apply products** in the sauna.
- **Don't change the heater controls** in a shared sauna. Ask staff.
- **Don't bring in glass bottles.** Use a plastic water bottle.

## Safety is part of etiquette

Looking out for each other is part of sauna culture. If someone looks dizzy or unwell, check on them. Don't drink alcohol before a sauna: the 2018 Mayo Clinic Proceedings review by the Finnish sauna research group warns that alcohol plus sauna raises the risk of low blood pressure and falls.
